# Albanian rebellion artwork — Crown and Vesuvius 0.17.26

Five different period images replace the congress picture in the five Albanian rising waves. A sixth image covers civilian aftermath. All 34 Albanian event variants are assigned; the Albanian charter decision also has its own image. Other regions retain their existing artwork.

The scenes span 1820–1913 and illustrate this alternate-history campaign. They are not records of wars against Bourbon Italy, and later illustrations are not evidence that their depicted events occurred in the campaign year. No event text, trigger, effect, population rule, war goal or decision cost changes in this art release.

Original scans and source-page snapshots are retained in the workspace. Images were cropped/resized and encoded as native DDS textures; original colors were retained. Event files are 521×203; the decision picture is 256×256. The native DirectX loader reports block-padded event dimensions of 524×204. No AI-generated image was used.

## Installation and validation

Faith and Frontier remains at 4.5.51 and stays a separate mod. The bounded installer backs up replaced files and records additions. It writes only Crown and Vesuvius and its launcher descriptor. Existing saves work without editing; restart the game to load the new picture files. Baseline GFM, flag caches, map data, save files and FF are not modified.

Validation compares parsed event/decision trees with the installed release after removing only picture fields, verifies every assigned asset and native texture load, checks repeated build hashes and verifies the packaged archive. Native decision and modifier-reference checks are retained. No live-game testing was performed.
