# Italy 1871–1900: artwork credits

Twelve new historical images supply event and decision DDS assets. Originals or normal Commons previews and file-page snapshots are retained in source_art. Cropping, resizing and DDS encoding were applied; no AI-generated art was used. Dates identify the source image, not necessarily the event year. Reuse is illustrative and does not imply endorsement.

- **cvs5_navy** — L'Illustrazione Italiana, 1 June 1879. [Caio Duilio (ship, 1880) - Trials - L'Illustrazione Italiana.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caio_Duilio_%28ship%2C_1880%29_-_Trials_-_L%27Illustrazione_Italiana.png). Public domain. Caio Duilio during fitting-out/trials; naval industry and diplomacy.

- **cvs5_bank** — Dante Paolocci, L'Illustrazione Italiana, 13 May 1894. [Processo Banca Romana.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Processo_Banca_Romana.jpg). Public domain. Banca Romana trial; banking crisis and supervision.

- **cvs5_fasci** — Dante Paolocci, L'Illustrazione Italiana, 21 January 1894. [The insurrection of the Fasci Siciliani in Mazara.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_insurrection_of_the_Fasci_Siciliani_in_Mazara.jpg). Public domain. Mazara unrest; Sicilian social conflict.

- **cvs5_monza** — Achille Beltrame, La Domenica del Corriere, 5 August 1900. [Bresci killing.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bresci_killing.jpg). Public domain. Assassination of Umberto; used only for the Monza event.

- **cvs5_electricity** — Anonymous photograph, dated 1883–1926 on Commons. [Kamin Kraftwerk Santa Radegonda.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kamin_Kraftwerk_Santa_Radegonda.png). Public domain. Santa Radegonda chimney and Milan cathedral; infrastructure illustration, not a photograph dated precisely 1883.

- **cvs5_school** — Luigi Rossi, La scuola del dolore, 1895; Artgate/Fondazione Cariplo digital reproduction. [Artgate Fondazione Cariplo - Rossi Luigi, La scuola del dolore.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Artgate_Fondazione_Cariplo_-_Rossi_Luigi%2C_La_scuola_del_dolore.jpg). CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/. Social hardship and Catholic social policy; despite its title, this is not used as a classroom scene. DDS derivatives retain CC BY-SA 3.0.

- **cvs5_classroom** — Demetrio Cosola, Il dettato, 1891. [Demetrio Cosola The Dictation 1891.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Demetrio_Cosola_The_Dictation_1891.jpg). Public domain. Classroom illustration for elementary and technical education.

- **cvs5_oath** — Dante Paolocci, L'Illustrazione Italiana, 3 February 1878. [Solenne Giuramento di S.M. Re Umberto I di Savoia.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solenne_Giuramento_di_S.M._Re_Umberto_I_di_Savoia.jpg). Public domain. Umberto's parliamentary oath; succession and royal-security reports.

- **cvs5_migration** — Angiolo Tommasi, Gli emigranti, 1896; photograph by Sailko, 2017. [Angelo tommasi, gli emigranti, 1896, 01.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Angelo_tommasi%2C_gli_emigranti%2C_1896%2C_01.jpg). CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. Emigration and southern social policy. DDS derivatives retain CC BY-SA 4.0; credit Sailko and Wikimedia CH support.

- **cvs5_dogali** — Michele Cammarano, Battle of Dogali, 1896. [Bataille de Dogali.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bataille_de_Dogali.jpg). Public domain. Illustration for the Horn of Africa. The painting does not certify that a specific battle occurred in a divergent campaign.

- **cvs5_riots** — Achille Beltrame, Episodio dei moti rivoluzionari alla Foppa, circa 1900. [Achille Beltrame, Episodio dei moti rivoluzionari alla Foppa, 1900 ca.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Achille_Beltrame%2C_Episodio_dei_moti_rivoluzionari_alla_Foppa%2C_1900_ca.jpg). Public domain. Milan unrest and public-order choices.

- **cvs5_steel** — Società degli alti forni fonderie e acciaierie di Terni, 1900; Archivio di Stato di Terni. [Società degli alti forni fonderie e acciaierie di Terni, opuscolo pubblicitario, 1900 - san dl SAN IMG-00001417.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Societ%C3%A0_degli_alti_forni_fonderie_e_acciaierie_di_Terni%2C_opuscolo_pubblicitario%2C_1900_-_san_dl_SAN_IMG-00001417.jpg). Public domain. Period industrial publicity with plant views; industrial contracts.
